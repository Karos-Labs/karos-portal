# Instagram best practices (researched 2026-09-15)

Governs the Instagram agent. These are the base rules: the client's sector adjusts them, and what performs on the client's own account overrides both. See 11 Best practices as a system.

Tags: **HARD** = platform limit or policy. **HEUR** = heuristic with data. **PRACT** = practitioner convention, no data.

## 1. What the platform rewards now
- HEUR (primary): Mosseri's three signals — watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach (Instagram, 2025-01, https://www.instagram.com/p/DFFyRp-pINJ/). Sends per reach unlocks non-followers.
- Carousel second serve (primary): "If someone sees your carousel post but they don't swipe, we'll often give that carousel a second chance and automatically move to that second piece of media" — Mosseri (Yahoo Tech, 2024-10-21). Carousels "often get more reach than photos"; music adds Reels-tab eligibility.
- Views (HARD): since 2025-04-21 Views replaced impressions/plays and count repeat displays by the same person (SocialPilot, 2025-04). Views/Reach is a re-serve proxy.
- Originality (HARD): from 2026-04-30 accounts that mainly repost lose recommendations on photos, carousels and reels; reuploads hidden unless "significantly modified" (Tubefilter, 2026-04-30). Mosseri's 2025-12-31 memo: 2026 = label AI, rank for originality; "raw content is proof" (om.co, 2026-01-01).
- Quality floor (primary, stated for Reels): low-resolution, watermarked, bordered, "majority text" or already-posted media is deprioritised (Instagram, 2023-05-31). Apply to every slide.
- Hashtags: not followable since 2024-12 (Buffer, 2026-03-24); Mosseri: they do not improve reach (Instagram, 2025, https://www.instagram.com/p/DQRVJhYEYCs/); posts with hashtags got 31.7% fewer views (Metricool, 24.3M posts, 2026 — correlation); Instagram's own guidance stays 3–5 (Hootsuite, 2026-07-14). Ranking and search read caption, on-slide and bio keywords.
- SEO (HARD): since 2025-07-10 public professional accounts are Google-indexed by default — posts, captions, alt text, geotags (LYFE, 2025-07-10).

## 2. Carousel anatomy
- HARD: ≤20 slides (SMT, 2024-08-08); images ≤30 MB; video slides 3–60 s; ratios 1.91:1 to 4:5; the first slide's ratio locks all slides (Hootsuite, 2025-12-03).
- HARD: grid tile is 3:4 since 2025-01 (Oktopost, 2025-01-28; Kapwing, 2025-12-30): 4:5 loses thin side strips; 1:1 is padded. Buffer (2025-02-26) says 4:5 — outvoted.
- Canvas 1080×1350. Cover essentials inside centred 1012×1350 (grid crop); text inside 1000×1270, feed UI overlaps ~50 px top / ~60 px bottom (Veeso, 2026-08-05, PRACT). 1:1 only for a square client photo. Never landscape.
- Slide count: >10 slides get more reach; reach rate 3.32% vs image 3.30%, reel 3.15% (Socialinsider, 2026-01-09); carousels out-engage images (Buffer, 4M+ posts, 2026-03-24). "8–10 best" (UseVisuals via Creatorflow, 2026-08-24) has no method. Within the 4–8 cap: 6–8 educational, 4–5 proof/offer.
- Slide 1: one claim, ≤12 words (PRACT), a specific number or contrarian line, a face where brand allows (Sydium, 2026-01-19); legible as a 3:4 thumbnail.
- Font floor (derived, HARD for Karos): a 1080-px canvas renders at ~390–430 CSS px (scale ≈0.36–0.40); Apple HIG body is 17 pt, avoid <11 pt. So body ≥32 canvas px, ≥44 preferred; headlines 64–96 px. Contentdrips (2026-05) agrees; Attentionclaw's 20–28 body (2026-01-29) is too small.
- Contrast (HARD): WCAG 2.2 — 4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large (≥18 pt / 14 pt bold ≈ ≥66 canvas px), so body copy needs 4.5:1.
- One idea per slide, ≤30 body words (PRACT); slide 2 stands alone (second cover); one template, slide counter.
- Last slide: one CTA — save, send, or a question. Question posts +36.7% comments; comment CTAs +202.8% (Metricool, 2026).

## 3. Single image with a deep caption
- Use when one client photo carries the idea (proof, one-frame before/after, launch) or a carousel would pad. Otherwise weakest: ER 0.33% vs carousel 0.50% (Socialinsider Q2 2026); single-image reach −22%, interactions −25% YoY (Metricool, 2026).
- HARD: caption ≤2,200 chars; ~125–150 chars before "more" (Hootsuite 2026-07-14: 138–150; others 125). Hook in the first 100 chars, never emoji or handle first.
- Length: <30-word captions had the highest ER across 9.1M posts (Socialinsider, 2024-02-21) — reactions, not sends/saves. "Deep" = 100–300 words: hook → 2–4 short paragraphs → one CTA; keywords inside sentences; 0–5 hashtags last.

## 4. Formats ranked, and which funnel stage each serves
Ranked by saves evidence (carousels ≈9× the saves of images: Metricool 2026; Socialinsider 2026-02-20).
1. Educational / how-to / teardown carousel, 6–8 slides — expertise; saves.
2. Contrarian or relatable POV, 4–6 slides or single image + deep caption — attention; sends.
3. Listicle carousel, 6–8 — attention → expertise; saves.
4. Before/after, single image or 2–4 slides, real photos only — decide.
5. Proof / testimonial, single image or 4 slides — decide.
6. Offer, single image + caption — decide; lowest reach; ≤1 in 5 posts.
- Rival IQ (2025-02-25): carousels led in Alcohol, Finance, Higher Ed, Influencers, Nonprofits, Tech; reels led in Fashion, Food, Beauty, Home, Media, Retail, Travel.

## 5. Hooks
- Number + outcome: "3 changes cut onboarding from 14 days to 4."
- Contrarian: "Stop posting daily. Post this instead."
- Mistake: "The pricing-page error 80% of SaaS sites make."
- Relatable POV: "You booked the villa. Now the 6 a.m. light."
- Reads as AI/template: "Unlock", "Elevate", "Game-changer", "X isn't just Y — it's Z", rule-of-three, rocket emoji, "Save this!!" as the hook, stock laptop, gradient blobs, Montserrat Bold everywhere.

## 6. Writing and design rules
- Voice: the client's, complete sentences, a fact per paragraph; never mention review or approval.
- No-AI-tells: no em-dash chains; no "Let's", "Imagine", "delve", "seamless", "journey"; no "not X, but Y"; no "Thoughts? 👇"; no bullet captions on a carousel.
- Hashtags 0–5 relevant nouns, end of caption, never in a comment. Emoji ≤2, none in headlines.
- Alt text per slide, ≤100 chars (reported limit), key nouns first, include on-slide text; Google indexes it (LYFE, 2025-07-10). Topic phrase once in slide 1, first caption line, alt text.
- Typography: kit fonts, two max; kit logo byte for byte, one slide, never redrawn.
- Text over images: flat region or brand scrim; contrast measured against the busiest pixels under the text; never over a face.
- Images ≥1080 px, sRGB, no upscaling, no watermark or app logo (Instagram, 2023-05-31); one grade across slides.
- Generated vs real: nearly a third of consumers are less likely to choose a brand using AI ads (Hootsuite Social Trends 2026); Meta auto-labels C2PA/IPTC-tagged images, self-disclosure voluntary (Meta, 2024-04-05). Order: client photos → licensed → generated, generated only for abstract/object scenes.
- Real people in AI (HARD): never generate an identifiable person, customer, result or product. AI-generated people need the "AI-generated profile" label or reach is cut (TechCrunch, 2026-08-31). Photoreal generations get the AI-info toggle; keep provenance metadata.

## 7. Distribution mechanics
- Windows conflict: Hootsuite (1M posts, 2026-07-14) Mon 3–9 PM, Tue 5–8 AM / 3–7 PM, Wed 5 PM, Thu 4–5 PM, Fri 4 PM, weekends midday; Later (6M posts, 2026-04-21) carousels Tue 5 AM; Metricool (700M posts, 2025) 8 PM Wed/Fri. Default weekday 7–9 AM or 5–8 PM audience-local; client Insights after ~8 posts.
- Cadence: median brand 5 carousels + 8 reels + 7 images/month (Socialinsider, 2026-02-20); 2–3/week ≈19% growth vs 10+/week ≈79% (Metricool, 2025); never back-to-back (Buffer, 2026-03-24). Default 3–5/week.
- Stories: reshare each post; story replies +88% YoY (Metricool, 2026).
- Collabs: up to 5 collaborators; photos, carousels, reels (Hootsuite, 2025-04-29).
- Trial reels: reels only, non-followers, metrics ~24 h, auto-share option at 72 h (Instagram, 2024-12-10). No carousel trial exists.
- TikTok: photo posts ≤35 images, 1080×1920 (Hootsuite, 2026-09-02); IG carousels get 4.7× views, 5.8× interactions of TikTok carousels (Metricool, 2026). Re-render 4:5 → 9:16 with brand bleed, add sound, hook in top 60%.

## 9. What to measure and benchmarks
- Primary: sends/reach (≥1%), saves/reach, Views/Reach, profile taps, follows. Secondary: likes, comments.
- ER by followers, Q2 2026: carousels 0.50%, reels 0.48%, images 0.33%; all 0.45%, −24% YoY (Socialinsider, 35M posts).
- Carousel medians per band (Socialinsider, 2026-02-20): 1–5K 993 views / 1 save; 10–50K 4,275 / 8; 50–100K 11,597 / 35; 100K–1M 35,370 / 98.
- Industry medians (Rival IQ, 2025-02-25): all 0.36%; Beauty 0.14% to Higher Ed 2.10%.
- Influencer ER, (likes+comments)/views (HypeAuditor, 2026-08-07): 1–5K 4–5%; 5–50K 2.5–3.5%; 500K+ 1–2%; brands 0.5–1%.
- Completion: no per-slide metric exists; Sydium's ≥65% / ≥55% targets (2026-01-19) are own data.
- Only 21% of accounts under 10K grew in 2026 (Metricool, 2026).

## 10. Pre-delivery checklist
1. 4:5, 1080×1350, every slide identical.
2. Cover inside centred 1012×1350; no text in top 50 / bottom 60 px.
3. Body ≥32 canvas px, headline ≥64; two kit fonts.
4. Every text block ≥4.5:1 against pixels beneath.
5. Slide 1 ≤12 words, one claim, topic keyword; slide 2 stands alone.
6. One idea per slide, ≤30 body words; last slide one CTA.
7. Logo = kit SVG, unaltered, one slide.
8. Images ≥1080 px, sRGB, no watermark, no upscaling.
9. No generated real person, customer, result or product; AI-info set for photoreal.
10. Hook ≤100 chars, not emoji-first; caption ≤2,200; 0–5 hashtags last.
11. Alt text per slide ≤100 chars.
12. Tells pass; no review language; licence record for sourced images.
13. Legible at 390 px and as a 3:4 thumbnail; ≥1 day from previous post.

## 11. Feedback for the developer integrating this
- Research: pull the client's last 20 posts; compute sends/reach and saves/reach per format.
- Decide: format = argmax(sends/reach + saves/reach) over client history, constrained by topic (proof → single or ≤4 slides; how-to → 6–8). Under 8 posts → carousel, 6 slides. Log the reason.
- Vet gates: no real-person generation, no fabricated proof, licence present, disclaimer, kit logo.
- Draft: slide 1 and the first 100 caption chars first; word caps and the tells list as a lint, not a prompt hint.
- Render: one template per client, safe-zone masks, font floor, contrast on real pixels. Visual QA: previews at 390 px and 3:4; fail on any checklist line.
- HARD: dimensions, slide cap, caption/hashtag limits, contrast, no AI people, originality, safe zone, font floor, disclaimers. Client may override within limits: slides 4–8, hashtags 0–5, CTA type, tone, window, hero photo.
- Learning loop: log format, slide count, hook pattern, image source, time; at 72 h and 7 d log Views, Reach, saves, sends, follows. Recompute monthly; re-rank hooks by sends/reach.
- "Format by performance": a bandit over {carousel-edu, carousel-pov, single-deep}, reward = sends/reach, 20% exploration, 90-day decay; topic limits the arm set.
