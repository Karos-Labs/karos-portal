# Humanizer and anti-slop tooling (researched 2026-09-15)

## 1. What "not sounding like AI" means, measurably

(a) **Surface tells**: the catalogue Wikipedia editors built from thousands of flagged drafts: inflated significance, vague attribution, promotional adjectives, "delve"/"underscore", "not just X but Y", "-ing" trailing clauses, em-dash overuse, rule-of-three lists, curly quotes, emoji bullets. The guide says humans spot AI text "no better than random chance" and single signs prove nothing ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)). Kobak et al. found LLM traces in at least 13.5% of 2024 PubMed abstracts from "excess vocabulary" alone ([Science Advances, 2025-07-02](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07016)). (b) **Low diversity**: instruction-tuned 2025 models, GPT-4o included, rank lowest on lexical diversity, below humans and 2023 base models ([Gude et al., 2026-05-07](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06030v1)). Across 11 LLMs, chat variants cluster together stylistically, model matters more than decoding, and genre matters more than whether the author is human or machine ([Rallapalli et al., 2026-04-15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14111)). (c) **Slop as substance**: expert annotators tie "slop" to coherence and relevance, not only vocabulary ([Shaib et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19163)). A word list catches (a); a style spec plus the client's own posts addresses (b); only a brief with a real fact per post fixes (c). Detector scores measure none of these.

Notes. **Quality evidence is thin everywhere.** No independent benchmark measures meaning preservation or brand-voice fidelity for any commercial humanizer. The most candid vendor comparison admits its bias and still scores most tools 2–3/5 on key-term preservation and 9 of 12 at 1.5–3.5/5 on grammar ([ProofreaderPro, 2026-08-10](https://proofreaderpro.ai/blog/best-ai-humanizers-2026)). ToHuman's round-up calls itself "not a head-to-head bypass-rate benchmark" and reports only its own latency: median 3.3 s under 50 words, 9.6 s at 50–149 words, n=20,741 ([2026-08-04](https://tohuman.io/ai-humanizer-api)). **Academic results on paraphrase humanization**: detector-guided paraphrasing cuts detection 87.88% on average "with mostly a slight degradation in text quality" ([Cheng et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07001)); iterative paraphrasing yields "semantic displacement with preserved generation patterns", meaning drifts while the machine fingerprint stays ([PADBen, 2025-11-01](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00416)). **Evasion decays**: Pangram 4 still flags 97.67% of output from 13 commercial humanizers ([2026-07-29](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27183)); Rephrasy's changelog shows the moving target: GPTZero first-pass 34% → 67% → "9 of 10" across v2, v3 (2026-01-30) and v4 (2026-08-10) ([blog](https://www.rephrasy.ai/blog)). Evasion buys months, not a capability.

**Should a detector score gate delivery? No.** A 7–17% false-positive rate means one honest post in 6 to 14 fails, and short social copy is where every detector is weakest by its own admission. Detectors penalise non-native and plain English ([Liang et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02819)), and Karos writes French for several clients. Scores are detector-specific: on the same Surfer-humanized text ZeroGPT fell to 39% while Originality stayed at 100% ([Originality's own test, 2026-01-22, n=1](https://originality.ai/blog/surfer-humanizer-review)). A 26-study review concludes detection "should be treated as one supportive signal rather than a stand-alone judgment" ([Aug 2026](https://www.etasr.com/index.php/ETASR/article/view/20040)). Log the score per post and detector; alert on a client-level trend; never block.

## 4. The quality-first alternative (style spec, few-shot, slop lint, model choice) with evidence

**Style spec + few-shot from the client's posts.** Genre outweighs authorship and chat models cluster together ([Rallapalli et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14111)), so the lever is specifying genre and voice with 5–10 of the client's own posts in the prompt plus a negative list. Four vendors sell "voice from a sample" with no fidelity data; Karos already holds the client's posts in its context docs.

**Deterministic slop lint** (milliseconds, free, explainable). slop-lint 0.8.0: MIT, zero-dep, exports `lintText`, em-dash hard fail, ~100 words, ~45 phrases, curly quotes, emoji ([2026-06-10](https://libraries.io/npm/slop-lint)). AntiSlop: 17 rules, `antislop.config.json` with custom regex and severities, JSON output; 35.0% hit rate on generated text vs 7.6% on 101k lines of human prose ([GitHub](https://github.com/javidjamae/AntiSlop)). Slopster ships as Vale rules ([GitHub](https://github.com/t0ddharris/slopster)). EQ-Bench's slop score weights 60% slop words, 25% not-x-but-y, 15% trigrams ([method](https://eqbench.com/slop-score.html)). Gap: awesome-slop lists Korean, Russian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Filipino packs and **no French list** ([GitHub](https://github.com/hwajongpark/awesome-slop)); Karos must author one and add sentence-length variance and rule-of-three counters, which none of these ship. Lint catches surface tells, not the empty sentence that experts mean by slop.

**Model choice.** The evidence does not support picking a vendor for "fewer tells". Detectability contradicts across detectors: Pangram 4 finds Claude the *easiest* family (FNR 0.195% vs Gemini 0.498%, [model card](https://www.pangram.com/research/model-card/pangram-4)); Winston catches Claude 3.5 least (66% vs GPT-4o 80%); GPTZero 86.7% vs 90.4% ([fast.io](https://fast.io/resources/gptzero-ai-detector-review-2026/)); Originality misses GPT-5. All current instruction-tuned models share the diversity loss ([Gude et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06030v1)). Keep the Anthropic models already wired in and spend the effort on the prompt; A/B with a rubric before switching family.

## 5. Recommendation and integration design

**First tool: Rephrasy API**, behind a per-client flag, default off. It is the only candidate with (1) a markdown/HTML mode that rewrites text nodes and leaves links, hashtags and CTAs byte-for-byte ([2026-07-02](https://www.rephrasy.ai/blog/the-ai-humanizer-api-that-keeps-your-html-intact)), (2) a per-client Writing Style ID trained on the client's own sentences rather than a tone enum, (3) a readability score in the response, (4) flat per-call pricing suited to 100-word posts, no rate limits, EU jurisdiction and deletion on request ([API](https://www.rephrasy.ai/api-solution), [privacy](https://www.rephrasy.ai/privacy-policy)). Risks: small vendor, unnamed third-party LLMs, self-reported quality. **Runner-up: WriteHuman**: documented REST, seven tones, French, per-sentence detect endpoint, but ~20 s latency and no voice cloning ([API](https://writehuman.ai/api)). StealthGPT is cheapest but tone control is academic. Avoid Undetectable.ai: perpetual content licence, ~$1/1k.

**Pipeline** (inside agent-engine's draft step, after the last AI step and before the deliverable is written; for text deliverables a portal-side post-process before the asset is created is the quick path):
1. Draft with style spec, few-shot client posts and negative list.
2. `slopLint(draft)`, pure TS, <10 ms. On hits: one targeted revision by the same model with the findings as instructions. Cheaper and safer than a paraphraser; most clients need nothing more.
3. Optional Rephrasy pass (`input_format: "markdown"`, `model: <client Style ID>`, 10 s timeout, fall back to step 2).
4. Detector score as telemetry: Sapling per post, Pangram or Originality on a monthly 5% sample. Stored on the job record; never a gate.
5. Re-lint (paraphrasers reintroduce tells) and a drift check: every number, name, URL, price and date in the draft must survive; embedding cosine below 0.85 fails closed to the step-2 text.
6. Deliver; bill the humanizer call as a credit line.

**Cost per 150-word post**, on top of the Claude draft: Rephrasy ≈ $0.015 word-based or ≈ $0.05 flat-call; Sapling ≈ $0.005; lint $0. Total ≈ $0.02–0.06; Originality instead of Sapling adds ≈ $0.024; Pangram audit $20/month flat.

**Risks.** Fact drift (PADBen's "semantic displacement"): caught by the entity diff in step 5. Voice drift (a paraphraser flattens toward its own house style, the homogenisation in section 1): caught only by the blind test below. Evasion decay: treat scores as trend data. Reputational: never surface "humanizer" or "detector" wording client-side; the category markets itself as "bypass".

## 6. Test plan before rollout

Per pilot client. Control: 50 posts the client wrote and published. Treatments on 50 fresh briefs: A raw draft; B draft + lint + model revision; C = B + Rephrasy with the client's Style ID; D = B + StealthGPT standard. Three blind raters (the client plus two staff) see shuffled posts and answer "sounds like us" (yes/no) and rate 1–5. Automatic measures per post: lint hits, entity and number retention (must be 100%), embedding similarity to draft, Sapling and Pangram scores (descriptive only). Ship C only if it beats B on "sounds like us" by at least 10 points with no lost facts; if B lands within 10 points of the control, ship B and leave the flag off. Repeat quarterly; models and detectors both move.

## 7. Feedback for the developer integrating this

- Put `slopLint` in `src/lib/` as a pure, client-safe module (like `credits.ts`) with per-language packs (EN now, FR authored by us) and a per-client allowlist for brand terms; return `{rule, span, severity}` so the revision prompt can quote findings.
- Execution belongs in agent-engine's draft step (the only executor since 2026-09-02); text-only deliverables (X, LinkedIn, Reddit, newsletter, blog) can also be post-processed portal-side in `materialize.ts` before the asset is written. Instagram and TikTok text must be humanized before render. Markdown mode keeps hashtags and URLs intact but bills 2.5× words.
- Store the Rephrasy Style ID and `humanizerEnabled` on the client record; train the style from the curated context docs, and do not retrain `profileSource: "lab"` clients unless asked.
- Timeouts and fallbacks are mandatory: every vendor site in this research threw a 403 or 429 at least once. Reddit stays draft-only, one reply per run. Detector scores land on the `jobs` mirror as telemetry; nothing reads them to block delivery.
