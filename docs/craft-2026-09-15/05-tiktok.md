# TikTok and Reels best practices (researched 2026-09-15)

Governs the three TikTok agents (clipping, editing, content design) and Instagram Reels. These are the base rules: the client's sector adjusts them, and what performs on the client's own account overrides both. See 11 Best practices as a system.

**HARD** = platform rule/policy. **HEUR** = evidence-backed default the client layer may override. Every number carries a source key; keys resolve to URL + date below.

## 1. What the platform rewards now
- Ranking: interactions "which may include the time spent watching a video, are generally weighted more heavily" (TT-REC-FAQ); finishing "a longer video from beginning to end" outweighs weak signals; follower count and past hits are "not direct factors" (TT-REC-2020). **HARD**: reused material "without anything new" is FYF-ineligible (TT-CG).
- Attention window: average TikTok watch time 3.75 s (4.72 s in 2024, −20.4%); average video 41 s, only 4% watched in full (MC-25).
- Shares/saves: TikTok shares +13% YoY (SI-ALL); Reels lead shares at every account size (MC-26). Instagram predicts "reshare a reel, watch a reel all the way through, like it, and go to the audio page" (IG-RANK).
- Search: TikTok search weights query match above interactions (TT-REC-FAQ); captions, on-screen text, speech, hashtags and sounds are indexed (HS-SEO).
- Sounds: **HARD** — business accounts use only the Commercial Music Library (1M tracks); "Businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage", organic included (TT-CML).
- Non-followers: TikTok reach −47.19%, views −25.44%, engagement +35.61% YoY; Reels views −59.15%, reach −21.86% (MC-25).
- Reels differences: watermarked, low-res, muted, bordered, majority-text and already-posted reels are recommended less (IG-RANK); >3 min not shown to non-followers (LATER, attributing Instagram); Trial Reels go to non-followers only, report after ~24 h, auto-share within 72 h (BUF-TRIAL).

## 2. Video anatomy
- **HARD** container: 9:16; TikTok ≥540×960, ≤500 MB, ≥516 kbps, ≤10 min (TT-SPEC). Reels: 1440×2560 recommended, ≤4 GB, ≤15 min for ads (IG-SPEC). Deliver 1080×1920.
- **HARD** safe zones: Reels — top ~14%, bottom ~35%, sides ~6% clear (IG-SPEC). TikTok ships template files on TT-SPEC; measured organic UI: top ~130 px, bottom ~250 px, sides ~60 px (KREATLI). Default to the stricter union: text between y=270–1250, x=60–960.
- Length by goal (sources conflict): completion highest ≤10 s (BUF-LEN); engagement peaks 0–30 s on TikTok (6.00% ER), 45–60 s on Reels (0.35%) (SI-VID); reach favours long — >1 min gets +63.8% watch time vs 30–60 s (BUF-SMT), views peak 120–180 s (SI-VID), 3–10 min most views (BUF-LEN). Defaults: Clipping 30–60 s, Editing 20–60 s, Content design 25–45 s; 60–120 s only for a story that needs it.
- Hook: "Introduce your content proposition in the first 3 seconds"; "Prioritize your hook in the first 6 seconds" (TT-CREATIVE); 63% of top ads state the message in 3 s (HOM). Triple hook — visual change, on-screen text, spoken line — inside 0–2 s (HEUR).
- Pacing (HEUR; no large-sample study found): visual change every 2–4 s; talking head cut or punch-in every 3–6 s; no static span >5 s.
- Captions: captioned video +12% view time (FB-CAP). TikTok: "display 5-10 words per second when using text" (TT-CREATIVE, verbatim) — treat as ≤2 short lines at once. Bold sans, ≥60 px at 1080 wide, in the 40–65% height band, never in the bottom 35%.
- Ending: CTA in the last 1–2 s, loop-friendly cut (HEUR). Cover: hook text in safe zone, OCR-indexed (HS-SEO).

## 3. Formats ranked, and which funnel stage each serves
Ordering is HEUR.
1. Talking head, claim + proof — expertise/decide; single-topic physio account: 35 videos, 32M views (MC-25).
2. Podcast clip + commentary — attention→expertise; must add "something new" (TT-CG) and not be "already posted" (IG-RANK).
3. Listicle/explainer with b-roll — attention/expertise; faceless accounts reach millions with 5–8 posts/month under 50K followers (MC-25); majority-text is downranked (IG-RANK).
4. Story arc (problem→diagnosis→fix→result) — expertise/decide; repair shop 1.6–1.9M views (MC-25).
5. Reaction/Duet/Stitch — attention; Stitch borrows ≤5 s and auto-credits (TT-STITCH).
Humor sits at attention ("before you sell, connect", MC-25).

## 4. Hooks
- Spoken: question, result preview, countdown, contrarian; TikTok suggests questions, countdowns, result previews (HS-ALGO); HOM adds audio-led, emotion-led, visual-novelty.
- On-screen: claim ≤7 words, one number where possible, no brand name in frame one.
- Reads as AI/ad/template (HEUR, reject): "In today's video", "Are you tired of…", "Let's dive in", "game-changer", emoji rows, "Wait for it", smile-at-camera stock opener, uniform TTS over a real person, "link in bio" up front, three-adjective stacks, hooks without a concrete noun or number.

## 5. Rules per agent
### 5a. Clipping
- Moment: one claim, number, contrarian line or story beat with its payoff inside the clip (HEUR).
- Length: RIVERSIDE says 15–30 s TikTok / 15–90 s Reels; reach data favours longer (BUF-SMT). Default 30–60 s, floor 20, ceiling 90.
- **HARD** rights: unauthorised copyrighted content "may lead to a violation"; repeat infringers banned (TT-IP); fair use/dealing varies by country, never a default. Client's own podcast: fine. Third-party: written permission or skip; the 5-s Stitch allowance is in-app only (TT-STITCH).
- **HARD** originality: clip + commentary = "something new" (TT-CG); bare re-uploads are FYF-ineligible and Reels-downranked (IG-RANK).
- Overlay: PIP/green-screen, client face upper third; commentary ≤2 lines; caption credit "Clip: @handle, ep. N" + 1-s on-screen credit (HEUR).
- **HARD** music: CML on TikTok (TT-CML); CML clearance is TikTok-only, so the Reels export uses original audio or Meta's library (LEGIS; IG-SPEC).
- Captions burned in, speaker-synced.

### 5b. Editing
- Silence: Descript has no default; its example target is 200 ms (DESCRIPT). Default: gaps >600 ms → 200–300 ms; keep pauses before reveals (HEUR).
- Fillers: remove um/uh/you know with "avoid harsh cuts" so neighbouring words are not clipped (DESCRIPT).
- Jump cuts on every removed gap; alternate 100%/115–120% punch-ins (HEUR). Colour: neutral skin, no stylised LUT — "DIY or not overly polished" wins (TT-CREATIVE).
- Captions on-brand inside the §2 band; graphics land on the spoken beat, hold ≤3 s; logo on the end card only.
- 15–60 s: engagement peaks 0–30 s (SI-VID); go 30–60 s only when completion beats the account median.
- Subtractive only: never alter the client's words.

### 5c. Content design (no footage)
- Script: hook ≤3 s → one promise → ≤3 beats, each with a noun/number → payoff → CTA; ≤120 words per 40 s (HEUR).
- **HARD** voice-over: realistic AI voice = "realistic audio" → label (TT-AIGC; TT-CG); Meta "will require people to use this disclosure and label tool" for "realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created or altered" (META-AI-1). A cloned real voice = "saying something they didn't say" → label and permission (TT-AIGC). The label "won't affect the distribution" (TT-AIGC).
- **HARD** AI footage: realistic scenes → label; prohibited: fake authoritative sources/crisis events, public figures endorsing or bullied, likeness of under-18s or private adults without permission (TT-AIGC). C2PA-tagged uploads are auto-labelled and the label cannot be removed (TT-AIGC; TT-C2PA).
- Stock vs AI: stock needs a social licence; AI b-roll for objects/places/abstract, never faces, hands or in-scene text. Faceless works (MC-25); majority-text does not (IG-RANK).
- Sound: VO −3 dB over bed; bed from CML or Meta library; a cue per beat (HEUR).
- Retention: shot change every 2–3 s, progress cues, payoff in the last third (HEUR).
- Quality bar: 41% of video pros use AI in production (WISTIA 2025-06-16); AI shots 3–6 s, cut before artifacts, never composite a real client into a generated scene.

## 6. Writing rules for captions and on-screen text
- Limits: TikTok 4,000 chars (HS-CAP); Instagram 2,200, ~150 visible (HS-IGCAP). Keyword in line one; 3–5 hashtags (HS-SEO; HS-CAP).
- Say it, show it, write it — all three are indexed (HS-SEO); search weights query match most (TT-REC-FAQ).
- CTA: questions +36.70% comments, comment-CTAs +202.78% (MC-IG). One CTA per post; never "link in bio" as the hook.
- No-AI-tells: §4 list, no em-dash chains, no "Here's the thing", no closing summary line, no hashtags mid-sentence.
- **HARD**: paid/gifted → commercial content disclosure toggle (TT-BC).

## 7. Distribution mechanics
- Cadence: TikTok recommends 1–4 posts/day (HS-TIME relaying TikTok); brands average 15.17 TikToks and 9.38 Reels per month (MC-25). Default 4–5/week per platform.
- Windows conflict: Thu 6–9 am, Sat 10 am–6 pm (HS-TIME); Tue–Thu 2–6 pm local, worst Sunday (SPROUT); weekend mornings peaking Sun 9 am (BUF-ENG). Use client analytics after 20 posts; until then Tue–Thu afternoons.
- **HARD** cross-posting: export clean — no TikTok watermark, no borders, ≥1080p, sound on (IG-RANK); re-score with Reels-cleared audio (§5a). Reels allow 20 min but stay ≤3 min for non-follower reach (LATER).
- Series: fixed hook grammar, numbered parts; a winner reposted after 4–8 weeks with a new hook/cover went 6.7M→3.7M views (MC-25 case, HEUR). Test on Reels via Trial Reels (BUF-TRIAL).

## 9. What to measure and benchmarks
- Views per post: 1–5K followers 350; 5–10K 945; 10–50K 3,240; 50–100K 9,900; 100K–1M 34,900 (SI-TT). Metricool plays: <1K 1,526; 1–10K 8,532; 10–100K 33,835; >100K 170,018 (MC-25).
- Engagement: by views 4.20% (2025) → 3.85% (Q2 2026) (SI-TT); by followers 2.60% (SI-ALL); Hootsuite 1.5% (HS-BENCH); Buffer median 4.6% (BUF-ENG). Pick one denominator.
- Watch time: TikTok 3.75 s (MC-25); Reels 8.5 s (MC-IG). Completion: 4% full-watch (MC-25); hosted video <1 min 52% engagement (WISTIA) / 65% retention (VIDYARD).
- Shares/reach is Instagram's stated signal (IG-RANK); TikTok shares −7.77% YoY (MC-25), so any rise is signal.
- Follower conversion: new followers ÷ non-follower views; 24% of TikTok accounts moved up a tier in a year (MC-25).
- Retention targets (HEUR, third-party): ≥70% past 3 s, ≥50% at midpoint.

## 10. Pre-delivery checklist
1. 9:16, ≥1080×1920, ≥516 kbps, ≤10 min, no watermark/borders.
2. Text and logos inside the §2 band (top 14% / bottom 35% clear).
3. Claim + on-screen text + visual change inside 0–3 s.
4. Captions synced, ≤2 lines, contrast ≥4.5:1.
5. Audio: CML on TikTok; original or Meta library on Reels; no general-library track.
6. Rights: footage owned or licensed in writing; stock licence covers social.
7. Clip adds commentary and credits the source.
8. Any realistic AI visual or AI voice → AIGC label + caption disclosure.
9. No AI likeness of real people without permission; none of minors, private adults, public-figure endorsements.
10. Paid/gifted → commercial content toggle flagged for the client.
11. Forbidden topics and regulated claims cleared.
12. Caption: keyword first, 3–5 hashtags, one CTA, no §4 tells.
13. Cover frame: hook text inside safe zone.
14. Ends on CTA or loop; no dead span >0.5 s.
15. Length inside the agent's band, or a logged reason.

## 11. Feedback for the developer integrating this
- Hard layer (never client-overridable): container + safe-zone check on the render; audio source by account type; AIGC label + disclosure auto-added for any AI voice/visual; likeness guard; rights record per non-client source; branded-content flag; forbidden topics (existing guardrail). Client-overridable: length band, hook style, caption styling within the band, hashtags, CTA, windows, tone.
- Clipping — research: transcript + timestamps, score moments on claim/number/contrarian/beat and self-containment. Decide: 30–60 s, reject payoff-outside clips. Vet: rights (own/licensed/skip), forbidden topics, §4 tells. Draft: hook, commentary, credit caption. Render: PIP, captions in band, TikTok + Reels audio variants, clean Reels export. QA: checklist + "adds something new" assertion.
- Editing — decide: cut plan from transcript (gaps >600 ms, fillers). Vet: no word changes. Draft: caption + graphics timeline. Render: jump cuts with alternating punch-ins, neutral colour, end card. QA: checklist + no static span >5 s.
- Content design — research: keywords for speech/screen/caption; stock vs AI per shot. Decide: §5c script, 25–45 s. Vet: AI voice/visual → label; no faces/hands/text in AI shots; regulated claims. Draft: script + shot list. Render: VO −3 dB, shots ≤4 s. QA: checklist + label flag stored on the asset.
- Learning loop — log per asset: platform, length, hook type, 3-s retention, average watch time, completion, shares/reach, saves, followers per non-follower view, posting time, audio source, caption length, AI-label flag, format class. Re-fit length and window defaults per client every 20 posts; store overrides with reasons.
- Re-read TT-CG on 2026-09-24 and TT-BC quarterly.
