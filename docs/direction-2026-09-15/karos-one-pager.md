# Karos — how it works

Building a product is easy now. Getting seen is not. Karos is the marketing team for companies that have something to sell and no way to distribute it: AI agents that research, decide, and produce content for every channel, and reporting that shows what it changed.

## The flow

1. **Sign up** with your website.
2. **We study your business**: your site, your market, your competitors, how your audience talks.
3. **Your profile is built**: six documents (brand voice, market strategy, competitors, products, brand guidelines, target audience) plus a checklist of first actions. You can read and correct them.
4. **You run an agent**, with an optional note on what you want.
5. **The agent reads your profile** so everything it makes sounds like you.
6. **It researches the platform**: what your audience is talking about, what competitors post, what is trending, what already works for you there.
7. **It decides** what is relevant for you and your audience, trending now, and proven to perform.
8. **It checks** the idea fits your direction and never promotes a competitor.
9. **It drafts**: strong copy with a real hook and story, in your voice, never generic or robotic. For Instagram and TikTok it also designs the visuals.
10. **You review**: approve, or say what to change, and get a new version.
11. **Put it on repeat**: every Monday, every day, whatever cadence you choose.

Every run makes the next one better. What the research found, what you said in feedback, and how each post performed all go back into your profile. After a month, Karos knows your business like a team member.

## What you see

One home page with the full picture: how you rank in search and inside AI answers, what customers say about you, your audience across platforms and how fast it grows, how each post performed, and what the agents produced. Plus a plain-language explanation of how each agent improves your presence.

## The agents

**Up and running**
- **X**: one post per run, in one of five styles (build in public, knowledge, point of view, news reaction, quote), with a picture.
- **LinkedIn**: one post per run for your company page or for a person on your team, in their voice, ready to publish.
- **Reddit**: finds live threads where your buyers are asking questions and drafts two possible replies, checked against each community's rules. You post it yourself.

**Beta**
- **Instagram**: a ready-to-post carousel in your brand, from your own photos or sourced imagery. Also usable for TikTok.
- **TikTok Clipper**: give it a podcast, or let it find podcasts in your niche. It cuts the best moment, adds captions, formats the clip.
- **TikTok Editor**: give it your own video. It cuts the gaps, adds on-brand captions and graphics.
- **TikTok Producer**: give it nothing. It writes the script and builds the video from stock or AI footage, with voice and sound.
